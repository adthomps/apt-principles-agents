# Studio owners can now take all their bookings and clients with them in one download

**For:** owners of small studios (1–5 staff) who run their schedule in the app.

**Problem (illustrative evidence).** Owners who switch tools, file taxes, or hand the studio to a new manager have no way to get their data out. Last quarter's support log (illustrative) has 31 tickets asking for "an export". The workaround is copying the calendar week by week into a spreadsheet, which owners report takes an evening per month of history.

**What changes.** From Settings → Your data, the owner chooses a date range and downloads one ZIP with bookings, clients, and payments as CSV files that open in any spreadsheet. Exports of up to two years are ready in under a minute; larger ones arrive by email link.

**Customer quote (placeholder — replace with a real one).** "I didn't want to leave; I wanted to know I could."

**How to start.** Settings → Your data → Export. Only the account owner sees this option.
