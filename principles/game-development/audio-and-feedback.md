# Audio and Feedback

## Principle

Feedback closes the loop between action and understanding. Combine visual, motion, audio, and controller cues deliberately; do not use polish to hide unclear rules.

## Practice

Map important events to purpose: acknowledge input, confirm success, warn, indicate damage, reveal progress, or establish mood. Prioritize core-loop events. Keep cues distinct, timely, and proportional. Provide volume controls and non-audio alternatives for required information.

## Asset Discipline

Track source, license, attribution, format, size, loop points, owner, and replacement status. Treat AI-generated assets as requiring provenance and rights review.

## Evidence

Test with sound on and off, reduced motion where supported, repeated events, overlapping cues, and failure states. Players should understand what happened and why.
