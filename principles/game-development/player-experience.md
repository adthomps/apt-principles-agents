# Player Experience

## Principle

Design from the player’s changing understanding and feeling, not only from the feature list.

## Practice

Map arrival, first action, learning, challenge, recovery, mastery, and ending. At each moment record what the player sees, believes, does, and receives as feedback. Teach through safe action before text when possible. Make goals, hazards, affordances, and consequences legible.

## Inclusive Experience

Consider control remapping, readable type, contrast, motion and audio alternatives, pause behavior, difficulty flexibility, and failure recovery. Validate with actual players; accessibility is not proven by developer intent.

## Evidence

Watch without coaching. Note where players hesitate, misread feedback, abandon a goal, or form an incorrect model. Prioritize observed friction that blocks the core loop.
