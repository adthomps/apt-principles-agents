# AI-Assisted Game Development

## Principle

AI can accelerate framing, alternatives, scaffolding, content experiments, testing, and explanation. The human remains accountable for intent, rights, safety, integration, and whether the game is actually playable.

## Practice

Give agents a bounded question, exact project evidence, constraints, output contract, and validation step. Ask for small patches and explain unfamiliar code. Review generated mechanics against the design, run generated code, inspect dependencies, and playtest outcomes. Record prompts or decisions when they materially affect the project.

## Boundaries

Do not provide secrets or private player data. Verify licenses and provenance for code, art, audio, fonts, and training references. Do not assume generated payment, health, privacy, security, or platform behavior is correct; involve the appropriate expert.

Use several focused reviewer perspectives when a decision affects players, beginners, engineering, UI/UX, scope, and testing.
