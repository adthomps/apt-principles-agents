# Repo Usage

Principles explain why and what good means; skills explain process; agents provide perspectives; prompts and templates accelerate work; examples demonstrate; adapters expose assets.

Record owners, source links, assumptions, validation, and freshness. Use [APT principles](../principles/README.md) for decisions and [skills](../skills/README.md) for procedures.

Game development is one supported domain alongside payments, ecommerce, APIs, documentation, modernization, and service readiness. Its [principles](../principles/game-development/README.md), [examples](../examples/game-development/README.md), and [Game Product Hub extension](../product-hubs/product-hub-template/game-extension.md) support small, beginner-friendly projects without changing the authority of the four pillars.
