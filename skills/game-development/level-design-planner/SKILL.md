# Level Design Planner

## Purpose

Create a readable sequence of learning, challenge, recovery, and completion.

## When to Use

Use for a platformer stage, puzzle, arena, exploration area, encounter, or tutorial segment.

## Inputs

Level purpose, mechanics, player knowledge, target duration, assets, difficulty constraints, and playtest findings.

## Process

1. Define entry state, goal, exit, and one teaching objective.
2. Sketch critical path, optional space, hazards, rewards, and checkpoints.
3. Introduce, practice, test, and combine mechanics.
4. Plan readable cues and recovery from failure.
5. Build a blockout before final assets.
6. Define observations, owner, cuts, and next revision.

## Outputs

Level plan, flow sketch, encounter table, asset needs, test questions, and cut list.

## Quality Bar

Players can identify the goal, learn the intended mechanic, recover, and finish without author coaching.
