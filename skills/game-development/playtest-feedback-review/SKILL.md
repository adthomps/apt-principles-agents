# Playtest Feedback Review

## Purpose

Separate observed behavior from interpretation and prioritize the smallest useful changes.

## When to Use

Use after moderated, unmoderated, internal, or external playtests.

## Inputs

Research question, participant context, build, observations, quotes, metrics, bugs, facilitator notes, and known test limitations.

## Process

1. Confirm what the test could and could not establish.
2. Separate observations, participant statements, hypotheses, and requests.
3. Cluster findings by core-loop stage and severity.
4. Distinguish defects, comprehension issues, balance, accessibility, and preference.
5. Recommend the smallest testable change; reject unsupported feature voting.
6. Assign owner, evidence, cut list, retest, documentation, rollout, and rollback.

## Outputs

Finding register, prioritized decisions, unresolved questions, change experiments, and approval status.

## Quality Bar

Every recommendation traces to evidence and has a way to confirm improvement.
