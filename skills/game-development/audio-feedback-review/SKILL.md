# Audio Feedback Review

## Purpose

Ensure core events are understandable through timely, proportional, and accessible feedback.

## When to Use

Use for action acknowledgement, warnings, damage, success, failure, progress, ambience, or mix problems.

## Inputs

Event list, visual/audio/haptic cues, target devices, accessibility needs, asset sources, mix controls, and playtest evidence.

## Process

1. Rank events by player consequence.
2. Map each critical event to purpose and at least one non-audio cue.
3. Check timing, distinction, repetition, overlap, volume, and interruption.
4. Test sound-on, sound-off, and reduced-motion conditions.
5. Verify asset provenance, license, format, and replacement status.
6. Record findings, owner, acceptance evidence, cuts, rollout, and rollback.

## Outputs

Feedback map, prioritized issues, asset gaps, accessibility checks, and validation plan.

## Quality Bar

Players understand important outcomes without relying on a single sensory channel.
