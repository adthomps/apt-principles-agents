# Game Design Document

## Intent

Owner, player, promise, desired experience, evidence, constraints, and glossary.

## Play

Core loop, mechanics, controls, feedback, failure/recovery, progression, and ending.

## Content And Delivery

Scenes/levels, UI/HUD, assets/audio, architecture, target build, accessibility, tests, and documentation.

## Decision Control

Minimum prototype, cut list, risks, assumptions, next increment, rollout, rollback, and approval.
