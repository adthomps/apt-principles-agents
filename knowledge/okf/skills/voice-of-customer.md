# Voice Of Customer

Voice Of Customer is the APT skill for grounding product and roadmap decisions in customer evidence, explicit uncertainty, business outcomes, and launch readiness.

## Portable Concept

Use this concept when downstream repos need to identify the correct product evidence workflow without loading the full skill body up front. The canonical skill remains the operational procedure.

## Relationships

- Depends on the [APT Knowledge System](../principles/knowledge-system.md) for source-backed evidence and uncertainty handling.
- Can feed the [Cross-Repo Guidance Sync](../workflows/cross-repo-guidance-sync.md) workflow when customer evidence changes what guidance should be installed or refreshed.
